# ukraine-files-flyers
Promo flyer images for the Ukraine Files X Articles series

## Ukrainian Files

**Ukrainian Files** is a research blog by [William Aaron Turner](https://x.com/infinitumceo), President of Infinitum Corporation. It collects the sources, figures and named claims behind *The Ukraine Files* series of X Articles: aid and NGOs, sanctions and the Russian economy, the Eastern Economic Forum, In-Q-Tel, mobilization and intelligence.

- Blog: https://ukrainianfiles.blogspot.com/
- Full series (reading order): https://ukrainianfiles.blogspot.com/p/the-ukraine-files-full-series.html
- About the author: https://ukrainianfiles.blogspot.com/p/about.html
- X account: https://x.com/infinitumceo
- Infinitum Corporation: https://www.theinfinitumcorporation.com

The images in this repository are the banners and flyers used to promote those articles.
